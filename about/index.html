Our company

Engineering precision into every connection.

We started as a small hydraulic equipment workshop and now run in-house fabrication, welding, painting and assembly lines. Our engineering team handles OEM and ODM projects from drawing review to first-article approval, so custom units go from concept to serial production without switching suppliers.
