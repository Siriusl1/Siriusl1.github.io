How warehouse layout affects product recommendation

19 June 2026 · TUSKOLIFT

Two warehouses with identical pallet volumes can need completely different equipment. Aisle width sets the maximum machine footprint, and racking height sets the lift requirement — together they eliminate most of the catalogue before performance is even discussed.

Traffic patterns matter just as much. Mixed pedestrian and truck routes push projects toward lower speeds and better visibility, while dedicated lanes allow larger, faster machines.

This is why we ask for a layout drawing before recommending anything. The same product family can be right for one facility and wrong for another purely because of how the floor is organized.
